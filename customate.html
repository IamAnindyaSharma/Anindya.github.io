CustoMate: AI for customs clearance

An AI-powered product concept to cut delays, errors and compliance risk in customs clearance and trade documentation.

DomainB2B trade / logisticsFocus0-to-1 product designSkillsResearch, personas, BMC, roadmap, pricing, GTM
Summary. Consignments get stuck at customs because of documentation errors, wrong HS codes and fast-changing rules. I designed CustoMate, a platform that automates documentation, validates compliance and helps businesses clear goods faster.

01Problem space

Through research I found that many consignments get stuck in customs clearance because of documentation errors, incorrect HS codes and constantly changing regulations. A single export consignment needs several documents, and businesses struggle to keep up with the regulatory complexity.

02Target users and personas

  • Importers and exporters running SMEs
  • Freight forwarders and logistics partners
  • Supply-chain and customs compliance professionals

For each persona I mapped background, goals, frustrations, buying motivations and daily challenges, so the product is built around real needs rather than assumptions.

03Business model and value

CustoMate creates value by reducing delays, lowering compliance risk, saving operational cost and improving confidence in customs decisions. I mapped this into a full Business Model Canvas: partners, activities, resources, segments, revenue streams and cost structure.

04Product flow and user journey

STEP 1Onboard and upload
STEP 2Intelligent query search
STEP 3Verify and check compliance
STEP 4Collaborate and get support

The aim was to make a complex, regulation-heavy process feel simple and guided.

05Roadmap and differentiation

A phased roadmap from MVP to advanced features:

  • OCR and NLP-based document parsing
  • Intelligent search and query resolution
  • Automated compliance checks
  • ERP integrations and real-time regulatory updates
  • Analytics dashboards

Differentiators: AI-driven query resolution, multi-language support, end-to-end automation, seamless integrations and real-time compliance intelligence.

06Metrics, pricing and go-to-market

North StarA single measure of value delivered to users
Searches per userDepth of engagement with the core feature
Result confidence rateHow much users trust the answers

Pricing: freemium, individual and enterprise plans. The go-to-market plan covered target industries, positioning, acquisition channels, sales motion and KPIs.

What this project shows

I can take a complex, regulation-heavy B2B problem, break it into user needs, and design a scalable AI product that balances usability, technology and business impact.
