Zepto: scheduled delivery

A product requirements document to raise adoption of Scheduled Delivery among Zepto's high-frequency users.

DomainQuick commerceFocusGrowth, trust, unit economicsSkillsPRD writing, research, experiment design
Summary. Heavy users rarely pick Scheduled Delivery, even though it is cheaper to fulfil. I wrote an experiment-ready MVP PRD built on three pillars, trust, value and flexibility, planned as a Phase-1 A/B launch.

01The problem

Heavy users (4+ orders a month) rarely choose Scheduled Delivery. Three root causes:

1

Low trust in punctuality: users fear late delivery.

2

Poor slot visibility: slots are ignored or assumed to be full.

3

No clear value: no visible price advantage over instant delivery.

This raises delivery costs, strains peak-hour logistics and limits margin. Competitors such as Amazon Fresh and BigBasket already use guarantees and better slot signals.

02Goals and success metrics

AdoptionScheduled share among heavy users from 7% to 20% in 3 months
ConversionSlot-selection conversion up 25%; cancellations down 15%
GuardrailNPS at or above instant-delivery levels

03Research and validation

  • User surveys: high punctuality anxiety, low awareness of savings
  • App analytics: only 9% of heavy-user orders used scheduled slots despite healthy availability
  • User interviews: fears about lateness, no edit option, hidden slots
  • Competitive benchmarking: guarantees and price cues lift adoption elsewhere

04Solution: three pillars

TRUST

An "On-Time or ₹30 Back" guarantee on slot cards, with automatic wallet credit if the SLA is breached.

VALUE

A "₹20 cheaper than instant" badge using dynamic pricing logic, shown at the moment of choice.

FLEX

Edit the slot until 11 PM the previous day, reducing anxiety about rigid planning.

05Experience and rollout

STEP 1Browse as usual
STEP 2Discover slots clearly
STEP 3See trust and savings cues
STEP 4Track ETA or edit slot

Components: enhanced slot cards, an edit-slot screen, an SLA tracker service and automated wallet credits. The plan covered backend and API readiness, mobile QA, rider training and support readiness, with a Phase-1 A/B test in Bengaluru.

06Risks and future scope

Risks: SLA cost overruns, discount abuse and operational complexity. Mitigations: limit promos to low-traffic slots and cap slot edits. Next iterations: cart-level nudges, contactless drop and personalised slot discounts.
