EmoSphere: a safe space for wellness

A community-first social platform for young professionals who look successful on the outside but struggle silently with anxiety, burnout and loneliness.

DomainMental wellness / socialFocus0-to-1, MVP scoping, cold startSkillsStorytelling, personas, journeys, metrics, monetisation
Summary. Ambitious young professionals often lack safe emotional spaces, not success. EmoSphere is an emotionally safe, non-clinical community where people can share, vent, listen and reflect without judgement.

01Story-first discovery

I began with a narrative around Kunal, a fictional but realistic high-performing tech professional. His story surfaced the core insight: people feel pressure to perform, hesitate to seek therapy because of stigma or cost, and feel isolated despite being constantly online.

Existing wellness apps often feel clinical or demand too much energy, while social media amplifies comparison.

02Product vision and positioning

  • Not therapy, and not a replacement for clinicians
  • Emotional safety, anonymity and validation first
  • Community-first healing, without streaks, vanity metrics or toxic positivity

03MVP scope: SafeSphere

In the MVP

  • An emotional feed (anonymous or named)
  • Reflection Pods by interest or life stage
  • Emotion-first reactions such as "I feel you" instead of likes
  • Strong anonymity and moderation tools

Deliberately left out

  • SpiritAnimal, the AI companion
  • Reason: meaningful personalisation needs behavioural data and trust built over time

04Journey, roadmap and cold start

I mapped the full journey from discovery to quiet engagement and emotional sharing, tracking the user's emotional state at each step. The roadmap ran from discovery and MVP through private alpha, public beta and habit formation.

Ignition strategy for the cold-start problem:

  • Seed small, trusted micro-communities
  • Recruit early champions from tech, MBA and creator circles
  • Build feedback loops before scaling growth

06Risks and mitigation

I identified low engagement, privacy concerns, stigma, moderation failures and monetisation resistance as key risks, with a mitigation plan for each.

What this project shows

  • Using storytelling as a product discovery tool
  • Turning emotional pain points into thoughtful product decisions
  • Balancing empathy, growth, trust and monetisation
  • Designing platforms where user well-being comes before metrics
