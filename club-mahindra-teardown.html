Club Mahindra: app teardown

A complete teardown of the Club Mahindra mobile app from a user, business and product-strategy perspective.

DomainVacation ownership / travelFocusUX, conversion, retentionSkillsBusiness analysis, personas, UX critique, strategy
Summary. I studied how Club Mahindra makes money, who its users are and how the app serves them, then proposed practical UX fixes and product-level strategy to lift engagement, conversion and retention.

01Business and product context

I started with the company background, product offering and business model: how Club Mahindra operates as a vacation ownership (timeshare) business, its revenue streams, membership structure and long-term value proposition for families. This clarified the business goals the app has to support.

02Market position and competitors

I compared Club Mahindra with Sterling Holidays, Taj Hotels, ITC Hotels and Hilton Grand Vacations to see how it differentiates itself and where it stands on flexibility, pricing and experience.

03User segments and personas

I defined segments by age, income, travel behaviour and needs, and focused on three groups, mapping each one's goals, pain points and expectations from the app:

  • Family-oriented travellers
  • Senior citizens
  • Young professionals

04End-to-end app experience

STEP 1Onboarding
STEP 2Membership discovery
STEP 3Booking
STEP 4Offers and loyalty

I reviewed the app screen by screen, including resort information pages and referral features. For every major screen I documented what works, what does not, and where users may feel confused, disengaged or blocked.

Recurring UX issues: excessive text, unclear CTAs, little immersive content, limited personalisation, and missed chances to excite non-members and convert them.

05Product improvements

  • Better onboarding and clearer CTAs
  • Videos, virtual tours or 3D views to improve resort discovery
  • Personalisation using user data
  • Making members feel more valued through better rewards and experiences
  • More flexible membership and booking options
